Frequently Asked Questions

Answers & Clarifications

Is SATELITE.UZ an official exam testing body? −
No. SATELITE.UZ is an independent, original digital examination preparation and question-bank platform designed to help students master academic concepts through simulated testing and analytics.
Are answer explanations available immediately? +
In Practice Mode, explanations and correct answers are revealed immediately after you submit your answer for each question. In Exam Mode, answers and explanations are safely protected until the entire exam is completed and submitted.
How do administrators manage questions? +
The built-in Admin Panel allows educators and admins to create, edit, delete, publish, and bulk-import questions via CSV without modifying any code.
Can I use SATELITE.UZ on mobile devices? +
Yes, SATELITE.UZ is fully responsive and supports desktops, laptops, tablets, and mobile smartphones with custom-tailored exam grids and navigation.
